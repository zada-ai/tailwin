ABOUT

THUMPSTAR

Established in Australia in 2004, Thumpstar has achieved remarkable success in the off-road motorcycle industry, having sold 40,000 motorcycles worldwide. With 20 years of operation, Thumpstar has accumulated valuable knowledge, playing a pivotal role in the brand's success and global expansion. Presently, the company engages in trading activities across
various Western countries.

The dedicated team at Thumpstar shares a profound passion for motorcycling and actively encourages individuals to embrace the sport. Our primary objective is to make motorcycling accessible to all, emphasizing affordability without compromising the highest standards of quality.
